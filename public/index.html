How News Sentiment Analysis Works

Why traditional dictionary keyword counting fails on journalism, and how Gemini AI decodes contextual valence.

The Flaw with Keyword Dictionaries

Lexicon tools like VADER or AFINN count negative words in isolation. In journalism, words like "strike", "defense", "crash", or "investigation" are routine factual reporting. A space rocket test "crashing" into the ocean is neutral science, but keyword counters score it as a disaster.

The Gemini Semantic Engine

Our autonomous engine evaluates full journalistic tone, geopolitical weight, and systemic outcome on a continuous -1.00 (Severe Crisis) to +1.00 (Inspiring Breakthrough) spectrum. It recognizes that hostage releases are positive even in wartime, and democratic elections are neutral governance.
